Frequently asked questions

How is the middle spot calculated?

Where2Meet finds the geographic center of everyone's locations, then lists the closest cafés and restaurants to that point.

Why are the distances shorter than my real trip?

Distances are measured in a straight line. Actual travel by car, transit, or on foot may be longer.

The app picked the wrong place for an address.

Go back and choose an address from the suggestions while you type. On the results screen, the "Found" line under each person shows exactly which place was used.

I see "You're pretty far apart."

This appears when someone is more than about 100 km (60 miles) from the middle. Where2Meet works best when everyone is in the same city or area.

"Use My Current Location" doesn't work.

Make sure location access is on: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Where2Meet › While Using the App. You can always type an address instead.

I see "Connection Problem" or "Too Many Searches."

Where2Meet uses Apple Maps to search. Check your internet connection, wait a moment, and tap Try Again.

Does Where2Meet cost anything or need an account?

No. It's free, with no sign-up and no ads.

What happens to my data?

Nothing is stored or sent to us. See the Privacy Policy.
